Professional Internship Program

Our internship program bridges the gap between academic learning and professional work experience. Interns work on real projects under the guidance of experienced mentors, building both technical skills and professional competencies.

Professional Mentorship

Learn from industry professionals with years of experience

Real Projects

Work on actual client projects to build your portfolio

Certification

Earn a recognized certificate upon completion

Program Structure

Our internship is divided into focused phases

1

Orientation & Training

First week focus on onboarding, tool setup, and foundational training in the specific technology stack.

  • Company culture & processes
  • Development tools training
  • Code standards & best practices

2

Project Work

Core period working on real projects with increasing responsibility under mentor supervision.

  • Feature development
  • Code reviews
  • Team collaboration
  • Agile methodologies

3

Capstone & Transition

Final phase includes a capstone project presentation and career preparation activities.

  • Project showcase
  • Resume & portfolio review
  • Interview preparation
  • Job placement assistance
